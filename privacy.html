SourceVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Who uses SourceVault

SourceVault is a private, personal-use application. It is not offered to the public.

What data SourceVault accesses

  • Your Google account identity (name, email, account ID) to sign you in.
  • Google Drive files and folders that you select, used to display, organize, and annotate them inside SourceVault.

How data is used and stored

  • SourceVault does not modify, move, or delete your Google Drive files.
  • SourceVault stores your organization data (folders, tags), notes, highlights, and reading progress on the developer's server.
  • Document content may be processed to provide search and AI features. If an external AI provider is used, only content from documents you select is sent, and this is disclosed in the app.
  • Your data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train AI models by SourceVault.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SourceVault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can revoke SourceVault's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of your SourceVault data, contact the developer at josevillagracia.dev@gmail.com.
